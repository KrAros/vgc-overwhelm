Damage Calculator

Damage Calculator is useful because it provides a visual overview of all damage calculations by all Pokemon in the battle and is much more efficient than looking at the calculations manually in a 1v1 calculator. This tool enables you to import your team as well as select Common Sets which can be used to predict what moves your opponent may have.

Break My Team

Break My Team is a team building calculator. It enables you to input your team and ‘break its team’ by displaying the top counters or sweepers to your team. There are two modes: bReakMyTeam which shows the opposing Pokemon that can best attack all your Pokemon, and WallMyTeam, which shows the opposing Pokemon that can best wall all your Pokemon.

Use this toolGuide
Breeding

Breeding is an essential operation for obtaining competitively valid Pokémon. Thanks to this tool, it is possible to accurately calculate the percentage of a Pokémon's split with the IV, gender and nature desired; the calculation also takes into consideration the possibility of obtaining a Shiny Pokémon. It is a useful tool that will save you precious time.
